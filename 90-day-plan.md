# 90-Day Plan: Internal-Tool Builds → Vertical AI Agent

**The play:** Use paid no-code internal-tool projects for external SMB clients (idea #4) to
generate cash and pattern-recognition in the first 60 days, then productize the tool you end up
building twice into a subscription vertical AI agent (idea #1) in days 61–90.

**Operator profile this plan assumes:** Full-time employed in tech (Tech Mahindra), so ~10–15
hours/week of evening + weekend capacity, IST timezone, selling remotely.

---

## Phase 0 — Compliance first (Days 1–5, do not skip)

Working at an IT services company changes the risk profile of this plan, because your employer's
core business *is* building software for clients.

1. **Read your employment agreement** for three clauses: IP assignment ("all inventions during
   employment"), exclusivity/moonlighting, and non-solicitation.
2. **Get written consent.** Tech Mahindra has historically been one of the more moonlighting-open
   Indian IT majors (leadership publicly supported transparent gig work in 2022, with a
   prior-consent policy), which is genuinely in your favor — but "historically open" is not the
   same as "you have approval." Email your HR/manager describing the activity in neutral terms
   ("weekend freelance work building small business tools for overseas SMBs, no overlap with
   company clients or projects") and keep the reply.
3. **Hard rules regardless of approval:**
   - Never use company laptop, network, accounts, or work hours. Separate machine, separate email.
   - Never approach any Tech Mahindra client, prospect, or partner. SMBs only — businesses far
     below TechM's deal size (a 15-person dental chain is not a TechM prospect; a telecom operator is).
   - Anything you build on company time/equipment is likely company IP. Keep the wall absolute.

**Gate:** Do not take a paid project until step 2 is resolved.

## Phase 1 — Setup + niche + demo (Days 1–14, overlaps Phase 0)

### Pick the vertical
The vertical AI agent in Phase 4 only works if your Phase 1–3 clients cluster in one industry.
Pick using two filters: (a) an industry you've been exposed to through your work/account
experience, and (b) one with lots of small businesses, i.e. NOT telecom carriers or banks.
Good candidates for a tech-services background:

- **IT/MSP shops and small dev agencies** (10–50 people) — you speak their language natively;
  they drown in ticket triage, client onboarding, timesheet/invoice reconciliation.
- **Recruitment/staffing agencies** — candidate pipeline tracking, resume screening, client
  submittal workflows. Massive repetitive-task surface for AI agents later.
- **Healthcare/dental/clinic admin** — only if you've touched the domain; lucrative but slower trust.

Default recommendation: **small IT services / MSP / staffing firms**. Credibility transfer from
your day job is highest, and the eventual AI agent (ticket triage, lead/candidate qualification)
is an obvious upsell.

### Stack (~$100–150/month total)
- **Builder:** Lovable or Bubble (web internal tools), Glide (if mobile/spreadsheet-driven)
- **Glue/automation:** Make or Zapier
- **Data:** Airtable or the client's existing Google Sheets
- **AI:** Claude API for the agent features
- **Getting paid from India by overseas clients:** Wise Business or Payoneer (Stripe India is
  restrictive for this); Razorpay if you take Indian clients
- Separate Gmail/Workspace, a simple Carrd/Lovable portfolio page

### Demo asset (one weekend)
Build ONE polished demo tool for your chosen vertical — e.g. "client onboarding tracker with
AI-drafted kickoff emails" for agencies. A 3-minute Loom walkthrough of it is your entire sales
collateral. Don't build a personal brand website; build proof.

## Phase 2 — First paid project (Days 15–35)

**The offer:** Fixed scope, fixed price, fast delivery.
> "I build internal tools that replace the spreadsheet your team hates. Fixed price, delivered in
> 2 weekends, you own everything."

**Pricing ladder:**
- Project #1: **$1,000–1,500** (underpriced on purpose — you're buying a testimonial and a case study)
- Projects #2–3: **$2,500–4,000**
- Always attach a **$200–400/month maintenance retainer** option

**Channels, in order of effort/return for a first client:**
1. **Warm network** — ex-colleagues now at startups/SMBs, college network, LinkedIn 1st degrees.
   One message: "I'm taking on 2 small automation/internal-tool projects this quarter — know
   anyone drowning in spreadsheets?"
2. **Upwork/Contra** — distasteful margins but fastest cold start; filter for "Airtable",
   "internal tool", "Zapier", "dashboard" jobs in your vertical. Win 1, then leave.
3. **Niche communities** — MSP subreddits/Slacks, recruiting ops communities. Give answers for
   2 weeks before pitching anything.

**Timezone note:** IST evenings = US mornings. That overlap is your meeting window; protect it.

**Gate (Day 35):** If zero signed projects after ~30 real pitches, the offer or vertical is wrong —
change ONE of them, not both, and run 2 more weeks before re-evaluating.

---

## Phase 3 — Deliver, repeat, and log the pattern (Days 36–60)

- Deliver projects #1–2 (capacity-realistic at 10–15 hrs/wk: 2 delivered + 1 in pipeline by day 60).
- Convert each delivery into: testimonial + 90-second Loom case study + retainer.
- **Keep a "pattern log":** every feature requested, every workflow automated, every "can it
  also…" question. The Phase 4 product is whatever appears in this log twice.
- Raise price on every subsequent quote until someone says no.

**Expected financials by day 60:** $3,500–6,500 collected, ~$300 spent.

---

## Phase 4 — Productize into the vertical AI agent (Days 61–90)

1. Take the twice-built tool/workflow from the pattern log — likely candidates given the vertical:
   - **Staffing:** AI agent that screens inbound candidates against a job spec and drafts submittals
   - **MSP/IT:** AI agent that triages inbound tickets/emails, drafts responses, routes by urgency
2. Rebuild it once, multi-tenant, configurable per client (Lovable/Bubble + Claude API + Make).
3. **Pilot pricing:** $99–149/month, offered first to your existing project clients at 50% off
   for 3 months in exchange for feedback and a logo on the landing page.
4. Ship a landing page + the Loom demo; start collecting waitlist signups from the same
   communities you've been active in since Phase 2.

**Goal by day 90:** 2–3 pilot subscribers. That's not the revenue story — it's proof the
subscription product has a pulse, funded entirely by Phase 2–3 cash.

## Decision gates

- **Day 35:** ≥1 signed project, or change offer/vertical (one variable only).
- **Day 60:** ≥$3K collected and ≥1 workflow seen twice → green-light Phase 4. If projects landed
  but no pattern emerged, keep doing projects another 30 days before productizing.
- **Day 90:** ≥2 pilot subscribers → shift effort split to 70% product / 30% projects.
  Zero pilots but healthy project pipeline → you have a good services business; productize later.
