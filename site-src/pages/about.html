The founderAhren Powell

Why I make images.

I picked up a camera in 2012, when my baby brother was born — I filmed my mother giving birth to him.

When he passed later that year, the photos and short videos were all I had left of him. That's when I understood what images can hold.

Over time I fell in love with movies. They became the main way I spent time with my family — especially my mom.

Since then I've worked across all kinds of projects with many companies, and eventually took the leap to start my own. This industry can be hard to get into, especially if you don't know many people, or you're an introvert. So I wanted to build a space where everyone can feel free to create and be who they are — on their terms.

I love beauty. And beauty is art. And beauty is not a monolith — it comes in so many artistic forms.

— Ahren Powell, Founder
